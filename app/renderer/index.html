In-game hotkeys

Ctrl/⌘+Shift+X clear marks · Ctrl/⌘+Shift+H hide/show marks · Ctrl/⌘+Shift+M sound on/off

Keep open while you play. Drawings appear over fullscreen games and fade after a few seconds; your mouse and keyboard still go to the game. If a game hides the drawings, switch it to Borderless or Windowed fullscreen in its video settings. You'll still hear the sound cue either way.
