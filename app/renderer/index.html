In-game hotkeys

  • Ctrl/⌘+Shift+X Erase marks
  • Ctrl/⌘+Shift+H Hide / show marks
  • Ctrl/⌘+Shift+M Sound on / off

Drawings appear over fullscreen games and fade after a few seconds; your mouse and keyboard still go to the game. If a game hides them, switch it to Borderless or Windowed fullscreen. You'll still hear the sound cue either way.

Share your game

Click Share my game and pick your game from the list. puts the likely game first.

Then click Copy invite link and paste it to your friend in Discord or a text.

Join a friend

Got an invite link? Just click it. opens and joins by itself.

Got a code instead? Click Join a friend and type the 6 letters and numbers.

Draw on their game

Use Pen to draw and Pointer to ping a spot. Your marks show up on top of their game and fade after a few seconds (change how long in Settings).

Sharing? Open stream settings (the sliders button) and click Test drawing to see what your friend's marks will look like.

Change source

Your friend stays connected and sees the new source right away.
