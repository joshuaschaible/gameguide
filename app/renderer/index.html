Join a friend

Got an invite link? Just click it. opens and joins by itself.

Got a code instead? Click Join a friend and type the 6 letters and numbers.

Draw on their game

Pick a tool: Draw, Arrow, Ping or Laser pointer (it follows your mouse live). Your marks show up on top of their game and fade after a few seconds. Scroll over the stream to zoom in; right-drag to move around.

Sharing? Open stream settings (the sliders button) and click Test drawing to see what your friend's marks will look like.

Screen Recording permission needed. In System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen & System Audio Recording, turn on , then quit and reopen .
